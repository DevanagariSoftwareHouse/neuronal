\section{Lean 4: countability of natural-number pairs}
\label{sec:lean-bridge-08}
A pair $(m,n)\in\mathbb{N}\times\mathbb{N}$ can be enumerated
by visiting finite diagonals with fixed $m+n$.
Lean expresses this conclusion as a \texttt{Countable} instance
for the product type. The following proof asks Mathlib to supply
the product instance from the countability of its factors.
This certifies the countability claim, but does not formalize
the manuscript's particular diagonal enumeration algorithm.
