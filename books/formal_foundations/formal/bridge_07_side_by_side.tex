\section{Lean 4: two-sided inverses and bijections}
\label{sec:lean-bridge-07}
A function $f:A\to B$ admits an inverse $g:B\to A$ satisfying
$g\circ f=\mathrm{id}_A$ and $f\circ g=\mathrm{id}_B$
if and only if $f$ is both injective and surjective.

The forward direction uses the left inverse to show injectivity
and the right inverse to exhibit a preimage for each $b\in B$.
Conversely, surjectivity selects a preimage for every output;
injectivity makes that choice a left inverse as well.
This construction uses classical choice, a dependency recorded
by Lean's axiom audit.
