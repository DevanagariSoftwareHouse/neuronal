\chapter{Map from \texttt{matlab/path} families to arsenal theorems}\label{app:arsenal}
This map is generated from the repository rather than guessed. Every function in \texttt{matlab/path} has a specification in the method-specification chapter of \emph{Computational Electrophysiology}, which cites the identifiers below; the identifiers are stated and proved in the three arsenal chapters of this volume (Part ``The mathematics of the arsenal''); and \texttt{books/shared/theorems.yml} records the correspondence. The command \texttt{python3 books/tools/arsenal\_registry.py --check} fails if a function, a specification, a theorem, or the registry falls out of step.
