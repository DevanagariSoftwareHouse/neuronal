\section{Theorems without a dedicated implementation}
The catalogue also includes Kolmogorov extension, time-rescaling
goodness-of-fit, Davis--Kahan perturbation, Takens embedding,
and Kalman observability. Gaussian KL divergence and Gaussian
conditioning are now derived explicitly in the Gaussian toolkit These are
\emph{mathematical prerequisites or future checks}, not claims
that their complete scientific implementations exist in the
repository. The presence of \path{gaussian_process_smoothing.m}
must not be interpreted as a full GPFA model.

\section{Operational contract}
Read input shapes and units from the MATLAB function help.
Document sample rate, time stamps, channel orientation,
boundary conventions, electrode reference, trial alignment,
and the meaning of each null hypothesis. Benchmark a method
against simulated data of known ground truth before trying
to infer physiology from an empirical recording.

\section{From biological generators to filters and estimates}
The physical forward chain has distinct operations:
a cable/membrane source model supplies transmembrane currents;
a conductivity model with boundary conditions constructs a lead field;
\path{field_potential_forward.m} applies a \emph{supplied} lead field;
\path{bandpass_lfp.m} then uses Butterworth sections and forward--backward
noncausal filtering; spectral estimators operate on that processed trace.
Inverting a supplied lead field is regularized but not necessarily unique.
No software primitive converts a fitted spectral peak directly into a
uniquely identified membrane current.

\section{Model-fitting limitations exposed by the new chapters}
\path{poisson_spike_glm.m} uses a binned canonical Poisson model with
clipped linear predictors: deriving the ideal log-concave Poisson
objective does not guarantee that every numerical iterate of the
implementation maximizes the same unclipped likelihood.
\path{tensor_cp_als.m} is a ridge-regularized CP-ALS primitive; alternating
steps are not a global-convergence theorem. \path{granger_causality.m}
returns a temporal predictive log-variance ratio, while
\path{transfer_entropy.m} uses discretized first-order
conditional mutual information. The Gaussian equivalence theorem
does not claim these distinct implementations have matching finite-sample
outputs or identify causal interventions.
